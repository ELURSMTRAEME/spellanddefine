Privacy policy

Spell & Define · Last updated 28 October 2026

Spell & Define does not collect, store or share any personal information.

When you hold the microphone button, the app listens to your voice so it can recognise the word you say. This happens entirely on your Mac using a speech model built into the app. The audio is not saved and is never sent over the internet. Words you type or look up stay on your Mac.

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

If you have any questions, email simon@staddon.uk.
